Disease: Septoria Leaf Spot (Septoria lycopersici)
Crop: Tomato
Region: Karnataka, India

Sec. 7.1 — Visual Symptoms
Numerous small, circular spots (2-3 mm) with dark brown margins and tan-to-gray
centers, often with tiny black specks (fungal fruiting bodies) visible in the
center under close inspection. Spots are smaller and more numerous than early
blight lesions and typically start on the oldest, lowest leaves before moving
upward. Heavily spotted leaves yellow and drop.

Sec. 7.2 — Triggering Conditions
Favored by warm, humid weather with extended leaf wetness from rain, dew, or
overhead irrigation. Spreads via rain splash and overhead watering, and
survives on crop debris and nearby weeds between seasons.

Sec. 7.3 — Recommended Treatment
Remove lower infected leaves and any debris on the ground around the plant.
Apply a mancozeb 75% WP spray at 7-10 day intervals during humid periods, same
as the early blight protocol, since both respond to similar fungicide classes.
Switch to drip or base-level irrigation instead of overhead watering, and
improve plant spacing for airflow to reduce leaf wetness duration.
