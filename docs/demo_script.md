# Kisan Mitra — 3-Minute Demo Script

**Setting:** Tomato belt, Kolar district, Karnataka.

## Beat 1 — The problem (0:00–0:30)
Camera: a photo of a single tomato leaf with early blight spots, held in a hand, phone
visibly showing one signal bar.

Narration: "A smallholder tomato farmer near Kolar spots this on their crop. There's no
agronomist to call, and one bar of signal to search with. Even an app that correctly names
the disease usually stops there — it doesn't say where to actually buy the treatment, or
whether it's about to rain on it."

## Beat 2 — Photo to cited diagnosis (0:30–1:10)
Screen recording: open Kisan Mitra, take the photo, speak the description ("dark spots with
rings on the bottom leaves, like a target").

Show on screen: the diagnosis result card appearing — "Early blight — 80% confidence" with
the citation "early_blight.txt Sec. 1.1" visible.

Narration: "Kisan Mitra doesn't just guess. Every diagnosis is grounded in the regional
disease guide, and the source is shown right on screen — this isn't a black box."

## Beat 3 — Voice advisory in the farmer's language (1:10–1:40)
Screen: the Kannada advisory text rendering, with the English version visible underneath for
the judges.

Narration (read the Kannada text aloud, or play back if TTS is wired up): "The advisory comes
back in the farmer's own language, factoring in the weather — no rain forecast, so it's safe
to spray today rather than wait."

## Beat 4 — Market linkage, buy side (1:40–2:05)
Screen: the dealer card appearing — "Patil Agro Center, 2.4 km, copper oxychloride 50% WP,
₹180/kg, in stock today."

Narration: "This is where it stops being a diagnosis app and becomes an agent — it already
knows where to buy the treatment, today, at this price."

## Beat 5 — The standout moment: outbreak alert (2:05–2:40)
Screen: the orange outbreak banner appearing at the top of the screen for a second,
different farmer — "Early blight has been reported on 7 nearby tomato plots in the last 7
days. Inspect your crop today."

Narration: "And here's the moment no existing app does: a second farmer, whose own crop
hasn't shown any symptoms yet, gets warned *before* it does — because Kisan Mitra is
reasoning across nearby reports, not just one photo at a time."

## Beat 6 — Close (2:40–3:00)
Narration: "Built on Gemini, Vertex AI Search, Firestore, and Cloud Run — and it scales to
any crop in the regional guide, not just tomato. Kisan Mitra: diagnosis that doesn't stop at
diagnosis."

## Camera/screen checklist
- [ ] Real or printed photo of a blighted tomato leaf, in-hand, visible phone signal indicator
- [ ] Screen recording of the live app (not a mockup) running against the FastAPI backend
- [ ] Kannada text clearly visible and legible on screen (zoom in if needed)
- [ ] Dealer card and outbreak banner both captured in the same recording session
- [ ] Total runtime under 3:00 — rehearse with a stopwatch, Beat 5 is the moment to protect if cutting for time
