Reference: Healthy Tomato Plant Baseline
Crop: Tomato
Region: Karnataka, India

Sec. 9.1 — Visual Baseline
A healthy tomato plant has uniform deep-green leaves with no spotting, curling,
yellowing, wilting, or stippling. Leaf edges lie flat, not crinkled or upward-
curled. Stems are firm and upright, not sunken or dark at the base. Fruit sets
evenly without sunken end patches or greasy brown blotches. Growth looks vigorous
and bushy only in proportion to the plant's age, not stunted relative to
neighboring plants of the same age.

Sec. 9.2 — When to Still Monitor
Even a plant that looks healthy today should be rechecked after heavy rain or a
hot, dry spell, since early blight, late blight, and spider mite damage can
appear within days once conditions turn favorable for them.

Sec. 9.3 — Recommended Action
No treatment needed. Continue routine watering and monitoring. If several nearby
plots have recently reported disease, inspect this plot more frequently than
usual during that window, since the same conditions and sometimes the same pest
vectors affect neighboring fields.
